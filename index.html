How it works and privacy

This app replaces /attachments/posters/ with /video/ (or /lsvideo/thumbnails/ with /lsvideo/videos/) and changes the image extension to your chosen video format. It does not make requests to test videos, save history, or send your links to the app publisher. Opening a video contacts the LPSG CDN through your browser. The app runs offline once installed and cached.

Built for quick links. No videos are stored by this app.
